Academic Courses by Year

Explore the curriculum for each year of the BMS program. Click on a course to see more details (content will be linked from the curriculum).

Year 1

MAN 111: Gross Anatomy and Histology

Detailed histology and macroscopic anatomy of body structures, skeleton, muscles, and innervation.

Semester I

MBM 112: Biochemistry and Molecular Biology I

Cell biology, acid-base balance, carbohydrate, protein, lipid, and nucleotide chemistry.

Semester I

MIC 113: ICT & Communication Skills

Psychology of study, communication skills, reading, oral and writing skills, and computer applications.

Semester I

MPE 114: Professionalism & Ethics in Health and Research

Introduction to concepts and principles of ethics, integrity, and professional behavior.

Semester I

MPH 115: Physiology I

Body fluids, excitable tissue, blood, excretory system, circulation, and respiration.

Semester I

MED 121: Embryology and Dissection

Gametogenesis, fertilization, embryogenesis, systemic embryology, and neuroanatomy.

Semester II

MBS 122: Behavioural Science

Psychology, medical sociology, and anthropology. Personality, human learning, memory, death and dying.

Semester II

MPH 124: Physiology II

Digestive system, metabolism, endocrine system, reproductive physiology, and central nervous system.

Semester II

MBM 125: Biochemistry and Molecular Biology II

Lipid, amino acid, heme metabolism, molecular biology, cancer biology, and signal transduction.

Semester II

Year 2

MMI 211: Microbiology and Immunology

Bacteriology, virology, mycology, and immunology. Structure, physiology, genetics, and pathogenesis.

Semester III

MPE 212: Parasitology & Entomology

Morphology, classification, clinical features, pathogenesis, and laboratory diagnosis of parasites and vectors.

Semester III

MEB 213: Epidemiology and Biostatistics

Epidemiological principles, statistical measures of health, determinants of disease, and biostatistics.

Semester III

MHD 214: Health Systems & Development

Health care system evaluation, development studies, health policies, and district health services.

Semester III

MCH 123: Introduction to Community Health

General principles of community health, COBERMS, disaster preparedness, and community drug use.

Semester III

MCH 221: Community Health Practice

Community organization, assessment, diagnosis, food security, nutritional disorders, and environmental health.

Semester IV

MCP 222: Basic and Clinical Pharmacology

Pharmacodynamics, pharmacokinetics, drug interactions, and pharmacology of major drug classes.

Semester IV

MPA 223: Pathology

General and systemic pathology, immunopathology, chemical pathology, and disease processes.

Semester IV

MPP 224: Psychopathology

Basic psychopathological concepts, building blocks for understanding psychiatric symptomatology.

Semester IV

MHB 225: Hematology and Blood Transfusion

Blood disorders, identification, investigation, management, and application of blood transfusion principles.

Semester IV

Year 3

MMD 311: Management of Diseases

Principles of history taking, physical examination, and scientific basis of diagnosis and management of clinical conditions.

Semester V

MRI 312: Radiology and Imaging

Principles of medical imaging, different modalities, utilization in disease diagnosis, and interpretation.

Semester V

MPE 321: Professionalism and Ethics in Medical Practice

Ethical and legal issues in medical decision-making, structure and function of law in relation to medicine.

Semester VI

MDC 322: Communicable & Non Communicable Diseases Control

Knowledge and skills of disease control at the community level, magnitude, and management approaches.

Semester VI

MRP 323: Research Project Development

Research methodology, proposal development stages, and preparation of a research proposal.

Semester VI

MOM 324: Occupational Medicine

Causes, prevention, recognition, and management of occupational health hazards.

Semester VI

MME 325: Management and Entrepreneurship

Principles of management, health sector economics, financial management, and human resource management.

Semester VI

MFT 326: Forensic Medicine and Toxicology

Medico-legal matters, court procedures, report writing, death certification, and toxicology.

Semester VI

Year 4

MPM 421: Psychiatry and Mental Health

Clinical knowledge, attitudes, and skills in assessing, diagnosing, and managing psychiatric disorders.

Semester VII & VIII

MAC 422: Anaesthesia and Critical Care Medicine

Basic principles and practice of safe anaesthesia, resuscitation, and critical care medicine.

Semester VII & VIII

MOP 423: Ophthalmology

Concepts of blindness, low vision, and diagnosis and management of common ocular conditions.

Semester VII & VIII

MEM 424: Emergency Medicine

Basic principles of management of common emergency conditions in adults and children.

Semester VII & VIII

MGS 411: General Surgery, Orthopedic Trauma and Neurosurgery I

Evaluation, diagnosis, and management of common surgical conditions, with a focus on interdisciplinary approaches.

Semester VII & VIII

MIM 412: Internal Medicine I

Essential knowledge and skills to recognise, assess, and manage common medical conditions.

Semester VII & VIII

MOG 413: Reproductive Health, Obstetrics and Gynaecology I

Necessary knowledge and skills to assess, identify, and manage normal processes and disease conditions in RH, OBGY.

Semester VII & VIII

MPC 414: Paediatrics and Child Health I

Comprehensive knowledge and professional skills to recognise, assess, and manage childhood illnesses.

Semester VII & VIII

MOT 425: Otorhinolaryngology

Basic principles of ENT, use of medical equipment, and interpretation of laboratory and imaging results.

Semester VII & VIII

MON 426: Orthopaedics, Traumatology and Neurosurgery

Management of common conditions in orthopaedics, traumatology, and neurosurgery.

Semester VII & VIII

Year 5

MGS 511: General Surgery, Orthopedic Trauma, Neurosurgery & Paediatric Surgery II

Advanced management of surgical diseases, with enhanced clinical skills and decision-making.

Semester IX & X

MIM 512: Internal Medicine II

Advanced knowledge, basic clinical skills, and investigative approaches for managing common medical diseases.

Semester IX & X

MOG 521: Reproductive Health, Obstetrics and Gynaecology II

Advanced competencies in managing obstetric, reproductive health, and gynaecological conditions.

Semester IX & X

MPC 522: Paediatrics and Child Health II

Advanced management of childhood illnesses and health problems of newborns, infants, children, and adolescents.

Semester IX & X

MCH 513: Community Health

Fieldwork and application of community health principles in a practical setting.

Semester IX & X

MRR 523: Research Data Analysis and Reporting

Data analysis methods and report writing for research projects.

Semester IX & X

Research Proposal Hearing Scheduled

📝CAT II Examination Timetable was released.

The CAT II Examination Timetable has been uploaded and is now available for students. 📚 Examinations will commence from 20th July to 24th July 2026. Students are advised to check their schedules and prepare accordingly. ✅

📅 June 19, 2026

⚠️BMS 3.2 Students Raise Concerns Over CAT I Examination

Some BMS 3.2 students have expressed concerns regarding the Management and Entrepreneurship CAT I examination conducted on Friday. Students claim that certain questions covered content for which no lecture notes, reading materials, or reference books had been provided. Discussions are reportedly underway to present these concerns to the relevant university authorities.
