The Collaboration Story

BMS Hub was born from a simple conversation between Donant and Millikan during a late-night study session. Frustrated by the scattered nature of academic resources, Donsmile proposed the idea of a centralized platform where BMS students could easily access course materials, past papers, and important updates.

What started as a small project quickly grew into a collaborative effort. The development team worked tirelessly to design a user-friendly interface, while Donsmile and a network of student representatives gathered and verified resources from various sources, including faculty members, alumni, and departmental archives.

Today, BMS Hub serves as the go-to academic companion for over 500 medical students at KIUT, and we continue to expand and improve the platform based on student feedback.
